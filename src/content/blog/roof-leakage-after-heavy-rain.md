## Start with safety and containment

Move electrical items away from damp areas and contain dripping water where possible. Avoid stepping onto wet roof surfaces without proper access and safety equipment.

## Record the leak pattern

Photos taken during and after rain are useful. Note whether the leak appears near gutters, roof junctions, skylights, penetrations or ceiling edges.

## Inspection should follow the water path

Roof leakage is not always directly above the stain. Water can travel along beams, membranes or ceiling voids before appearing indoors.
