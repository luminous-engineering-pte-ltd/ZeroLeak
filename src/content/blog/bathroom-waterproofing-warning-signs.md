## Why early symptoms matter

Bathroom leaks can appear small at first. A stained ceiling below, damp skirting, hollow tiles or persistent mould may point to moisture moving behind finishes.

Early inspection helps separate surface condensation from a waterproofing defect, pipe issue or failed joint.

## What property owners can document

Take clear photos of the stain, note when it appears, and check whether it worsens after shower use or heavy rain. This simple record helps a contractor understand the pattern before recommending a repair.

## When no hacking may be considered

No hacking waterproofing can be suitable in some cases, but not every leak is a surface issue. The right method depends on substrate condition, water path and the existing finish.
