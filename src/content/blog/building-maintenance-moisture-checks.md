## Review vulnerable junctions

Balconies, roof edges, bathrooms, gutters, external walls and slab joints are common places to inspect during routine maintenance.

## Look for pattern changes

Repeated staining, peeling paint, mould return or damp odour can point to a moisture source that needs investigation.

## Keep records

Photos, dates and weather notes help identify whether the issue is tied to rain, plumbing use or persistent trapped moisture.
