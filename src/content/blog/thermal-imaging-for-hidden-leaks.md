## What thermal imaging can reveal

Thermal imaging may show temperature differences linked to trapped moisture, damp surfaces or concealed water movement.

## Why interpretation matters

Images alone do not prove the source. Sun exposure, air-conditioning, material thickness and recent weather can all affect readings.

## Combining methods

A practical leak assessment combines visual inspection, moisture history, property layout and targeted testing where needed.
