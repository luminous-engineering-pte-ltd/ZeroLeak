## Strengthening starts with assessment

Structural strengthening should be based on the member condition, loading concern and repair objective. A suitable method depends on the engineering context.

## Common system types

FRP systems, fibre carbon wrapping, flexural strengthening and column confinement may be considered for different structural needs.

## Clear scope is essential

The recommended system should define surface preparation, installation sequence, curing needs and review steps.
