## Cracks should be assessed in context

Not every wall crack requires the same repair. Location, width, direction, movement and signs of dampness all influence the recommendation.

## Moisture changes the repair decision

A crack that also allows water ingress may need leak treatment before cosmetic reinstatement. Covering it too early can hide the symptom while the cause remains.

## Practical next step

Document the crack and monitor whether it changes after rain, nearby works or repeated wetting.
